App icon concepts

Rough drafts in the app's dojo style. Shown large, at real iOS sizes, and on a wallpaper.

1 · Ninja drop

The companion from the app as the icon: drop, mask, red headband tails on indigo with a faint wave pattern. Most recognisable, ties icon and app together.

2 · Ensō + drop

A Zen brush circle around a small drop, with a red 水 (water) seal. Calm, dojo-like, works in black and white.

3 · Wave in a drop

A drop outline half filled with the seigaiha wave pattern, like the app's progress fill. Clean and on-theme, but less unique.
